Quotidian Share

Daily game scores with friends.

Finish a game like Wordle, Connections, Maptap or Globle, tap Share, and choose Quotidian. Your friends see how you did, as a little sticker on their Feed, instead of cluttering up the groupchat.

What's inside

  • Feed. Everyone's scores today as colorful stickers, with reactions.
  • Standings. A leaderboard for each game.
  • Stats. Streaks, averages and bests for every game.
  • Best. A card for each friend's best result.
  • Friends. Add people by @handle.

  • Connections

Private by default

Nothing is public. Only friends you accept can see your scores. No ads, no tracking.
