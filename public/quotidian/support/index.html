Common questions

How do I post a score?

Finish a game, tap Share, choose Quotidian, add a short message if you like, and tap Post.

Tip: to find Quotidian faster, scroll the row of apps in the share sheet to the end, tap More, then Edit, and tap the green + next to Quotidian to add it to your favorites.

How do I add friends?

Open the Friends tab, tap "Add another friend", and type their @handle. They get a request and choose whether to accept. Your own handle is shown on that screen so you can share it.

Why don't I see my friends' scores?

Scores appear only for friends who accepted your request and only after they've shared a game. Pull down on the Feed to refresh.

A score didn't post.

It stays on your phone with a red "!" until it sends, and it retries when you're back online. Tap the "!" to retry.

How do I report someone or block them?

Long-press a card, or tap the ... menu on a friend's profile, then choose Report, Remove friend or Block.

How do I change my avatar or handle?

Tap your avatar at the top right of the app.

How do I delete my account?

Tap your avatar, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, scores and friendships. It can't be undone.
